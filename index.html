How to play
Your goal: get admitted, pass your exams, graduate
  1. Move and look. Left side of the screen: drag to walk. Right side: drag to look. On a computer use W A S D and the mouse.
  2. Run and jump. Use the RUN and JUMP buttons at the bottom right (keyboard: Shift and Space).
  3. Follow the arrow. The card at the top tells you your next step and points to the building. Tap it to open your Student Portal.
  4. Use the action button. When you stand on a glowing spot, an action button appears (keyboard: E). That is how you study, attend lectures, eat and talk to students.
  5. Keep an eye on energy. Eat at the cafeteria or rest in the hostel when it runs low.
